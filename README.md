# Life Progress (人生进度)

A stylish life countdown & journal app for Android. Track your life progress, write daily journals, and share beautiful posters.

## Features

- Life Countdown: real-time countdown of your estimated life with a home-screen progress bar
- Life Expectancy Quiz: statistically-based estimate from your lifestyle and health history (fun reference only)
- Daily Journal: record your thoughts and words, review them anytime
- Shareable Posters: generate beautiful posters and share in one tap
- Bilingual UI: Chinese and English, switch anytime in Settings
- Background Music and Tick Sound: relaxing piano music and a ticking countdown sound (on by default)
- 100% Local and Private: all data stays on your device, nothing is uploaded

## Download

Get the latest APK from the Releases page: https://github.com/conligu2024/life-progress-app/releases

- Current version: 1.3.1

## Disclaimer

The life expectancy shown in this app is a statistical estimate for fun reference only. It is NOT a medical prediction or health advice, and should never be used as a basis for health decisions. All data is stored locally on your device.

## Tech Stack

React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Capacitor (Android)
